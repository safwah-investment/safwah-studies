# Granola Cup — Café Foodservice Pre-Feasibility

Bilingual Arabic/English investment concept for a dry single-serve granola cup designed for Saudi cafés. The café adds 120–150 ml hot or cold milk at service.

## Live page
GitHub Pages path: `/safwah-studies/granola-cup/`

- `styles.css` — responsive visual system
- `script.js` — AR/EN toggle
- `assets/cup-concept.svg` — three-SKU packaging concept
- `assets/service-flow.svg` — café service journey
- `assets/package-anatomy.svg` — cup specification concept

## Scope
Pre-feasibility only. Financial numbers are planning assumptions and must be replaced by supplier quotations. Shelf-life, sensory performance, food-contact suitability, hot-milk performance, allergen management and regulatory compliance require validation before commercial launch.

## Current investment gate
Proceed to prototype + 20-café pilot. Do not invest in a dedicated manufacturing line until product performance and repeat demand are demonstrated.
